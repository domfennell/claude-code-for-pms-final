# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

open the claude.md file

### 2.

What can you reach from here? List what's in this folder, what's in Rook's wiki, and what tables are in Rook's database.

### 3.

I just joined Rook Industries as PM for Rook Dispatch. Read everything in 00-rook/company/ and add to the CLAUDE.md at the root of this folder, what you'd need to know to help me do my job here: the products, the people, the vocabulary, where things stand. Leave the session scope block at the top. Keep it under two pages.

### 4.

give me the TL;DR. What's the "so what" with 4.2?

### 5.

I want a good understanding of the before and after. What does 4.2 look like in terms of changes when compared to 4.1? Crucially, were all the changes that were made in the Q3 roadmap? Conversely, are there any in the Q3 roadmap that were cut prior to 4.2 being shipped?

### 6.

taking a step back, give me a timeline of events so i can better understand pre-4.2 and post-4.2.

### 7.

help me understand the problem statement: i am an (X) doing (Y), but (the problem statement) which leaves me feeling (Z).

### 8.

in relation to the issues being faced by people in 4.1, i.e. when we were planning our Q3 roadmap, give me the problem statements in the following i am a (user) doing (what is the user doing), but (user's problem statement) which leaves the user feeling (blank). keep it concise and in a tabular form.

### 9.

which of the problem statements did we identify as ones that should be resolved by 4.2. Align this with what we already know about what was done into 4.2, and what was left on the table/in the roadmap.

### 10.

before we move forward. i want to get a read from you. how confident are you in your understanding of the problems statements, the data, and how the data correlates with the problem statements. give me your score of 0-95, and let's figure out how to improve your confidence together.
